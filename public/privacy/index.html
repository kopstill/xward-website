Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Xward doesn’t collect any data. Your countdowns stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, and the developer can’t see them.

Your data

Your countdowns, your settings, and the photos you pick as backgrounds are stored on your device. If you’re signed in to iCloud and iCloud is turned on for Xward, they sync across your devices through your private iCloud database, except the app icon you choose, which stays on each device. The developer has no access to that database. If you turn off iCloud for Xward, the device stops syncing new changes, though a backup of the device may still include them. What Xward already synced stays in iCloud until you delete it, as described below.

Xward receives only the photos you pick, not your photo library. Reminders are scheduled on your device, and widgets read your countdowns there. When you export a backup or share a countdown, the file goes only where you send it.

What Xward doesn’t do

Xward has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and it contains no third-party SDKs. It doesn’t share your data with anyone. Apart from Apple’s services, such as iCloud and the App Store, it doesn’t connect to any server.

Purchases

Xward Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and the developer doesn’t receive your payment details or your Apple Account information.

Apple

iCloud and the App Store are Apple services, covered by Apple’s privacy policy. In mainland China, iCloud is provided by GCBD under GCBD’s terms.

Apple shows the developer some technical information about Xward. If you’ve chosen in your device’s settings to share analytics with app developers, that includes crash reports and usage statistics. It also includes a week of records of iCloud requests, each with the time, the system version, the kind of request, and an anonymous user ID that lets the developer group requests from the same iCloud account in Xward, but none of your data.

Deleting your data

Xward keeps your data until you delete the app and its data in iCloud. A countdown you delete in Xward is kept, marked as deleted, so that all your devices learn of the deletion.

Deleting the app removes its data from that device. To delete Xward’s data from iCloud, go to Settings > your name > iCloud > Storage (or Manage Account Storage) > Xward, and tap Delete Data from iCloud. On devices that have already synced with that account, Xward also clears its local copy, including changes not yet uploaded, when it receives the deletion.

A backup of your device, in iCloud or on a computer, may include Xward’s data. To leave Xward out of a device’s iCloud Backup, go to Settings > your name > iCloud > Storage (or Manage Account Storage) > Backups, choose the device, turn off Xward, and tap Turn Off. Apple explains both steps in Manage your iCloud storage.

This website

xward.app sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics. Vercel hosts it and processes request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse. Vercel also shows the developer traffic totals grouped by visitor IP address or requested page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
